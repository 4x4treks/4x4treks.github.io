2027 SEASON FEATURE TOUR
ALICE TO OOMBI:
Kimberley Magic

Alice Springs to Kununurra. 26 Nights. No Trailers. No children under 16. Limited to 8 vehicles. ⚠️ WARNING: High-clearance vehicles and serious prep required.

26 June - 21 July. Only 3 spots available. 
AUD$13,790 for up to 2 people in 1 vehicle 

"About as far into the Australian wilderness as you can get"

An epic outback expedition from Alice Springs across the Kimberley, combining iconic destinations like the Bungle Bungles, Mitchell Falls, and the Gibb River Road with exclusive 12-day access to the remote Oombi Track led by a Traditional Owner.

➡️Iconic Outback Tracks: Tanami Track, Old Karunjie Track, Pentecost River crossing, Oombi Track, Kalumburu Road, and a section of the Gibb River Road.

➡️The Wild Highlight: 400km on the remote Oombi Track—rock crawls, deep water crossings, ancient art, and untouched fishing in Balanggarra Country.

➡️Must-See Landmarks: Bungle Bungles/Purnululu NP (2 nights), Lake Argyle sunset cruise, King George Falls, Mitchell Falls, Wolfe Creek Crater, and the  Cockburn Ranges.

➡️Comfort Included: Welcome dinner and accommodation in Alice Springs, station stays at Home Valley & Ellenbrae, and a farewell night's stay and dinner at The Kimberley Grande Resort in Kununurra.

This is going to be a difficult tour once we get onto the Oombi Track. This trek demands a well-prepared 4WD, capable of carrying enough fuel for up to 800 km (for the Tanami track & low range on the Oombi) and you must meet strict vehicle requirements. Personal fitness and stamina are essential to handle the remote and demanding conditions. Deep water crossings and large boulders will be encountered.

There is an option to join the tour at Kununurra (less $1,000 for this option).

Alice to Oombi Tour FAQs 

What's Included?

• First night's accommodation at the Desert Palms Motel in Alice Springs (excellent security)
• First night's dinner at Lasseter's Casino
• StarLink Wifi - internet when we're camped in remote areas 5 - 8 pm
• Pre-tour help and advice on vehicle and gear setup.
• All the arrangements and bookings for the tour (just get yourself, your gear and vehicle to Alice Springs for the first night's included accommodation and dinner).
• Full-time, professional tour guide/4WD instructor with a Cert IV qualification in 4WD training and current remote area first aid certificate.
• Mechanical assistance and tyre changing.
• Toyota Land Cruiser lead vehicle with all safety, recovery, communications and emergency equipment - Starlink and Garmin inReach (tracker, emergency beacon & satellite texting), winch, snatch straps, Max Trax etc (meaning you only need to carry minimal recovery gear).
• All camping fees; all National Park access fees; all Aboriginal Land entry permits.
• "On-tour" training over the 2-way radio. You will be given help and advice throughout the entire trip and warned of oncoming traffic and hazards. On difficult sections, your guide will be out of their vehicle to help you and instruct you personally.
• Accommodation at The Kimberley Grande Resort in Kununurra
• Final dinner at The Kimberley Grande Resort in Kununurra

What's Not Included?

• 4WD Vehicle
• Fuel
• Food (other than stated above)
• Camping gear
• Insurance
• Optional accommodation upgrades

What are the Vehicle Requirements?

• A high clearance 4X4 vehicle with low range.
• Upgraded, heavy duty suspension - even on a new vehicle. Standard suspension will not cope on the corrugations and dune moguls.
• One spare front shock (or shock in spring assembly) and one spare rear shock as appropriate. These can be the originals. We just need something that will fit your vehicle to get us out of trouble.
• Six Light Truck construction (LT) tyres with over 70% tread remaining and not over 5 years old.
• Fuel range of 800 km is required for this tour.
• 5- - 80 litre water storage.
• Good quality UHF radio base set (Uniden, GME or Oricom recommended) fitted into your vehicle by a UHF specialist installer, and a substantial aerial - 6.6dbi, 1 metre in length, with large 30mm base - not the slim springy ones.
• Tyre pressure gauge.
• Tyre pressure monitors (TPMS).
• Good quality air compressor.
• A mechanical checklist is to be completed and returned by a qualified mechanic at least 2 weeks prior to the start of the tour.
• A medical form is to be completed and bought with you in case of emergencies.
• A tent, roof tent, or camp set up that is EASY TO PUT UP AND TAKE DOWN. This should take no longer than 20 mins each time (this can make or break your trip).
• Vehicle Insurance - you will be required to make sure your insurance company covers your vehicle for off-road driving. NRMA/RACQ etc will not retireve your vehicle from remote tracks.
• Travel Insurance - as with any holiday, you must hold travel insurance in case of unforeseen circumstances. 

Where can I get Fuel and Supplies?

Fuel is available along our route, however, you will need to have a fuel range of 800 km for the longest stretch with no fuel stops. All care will be taken to check in advance whether fuel is available where we are going. It is advisable to carry an extra 20L of fuel for contingencies. Water and food is also available in these locations but fresh food is not always available and variety is limited.

Can I hire a 4WD vehicle and gear?

No.

How rough is the track?

The Oombi track is extremely rough. It is advisable to get some form of paint protection on your vehicle to prevent damage. We recommend Bush Barrier magnetic paint protection. You should also have good quality underbody and side step protection.
