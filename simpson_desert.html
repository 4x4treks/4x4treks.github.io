Cross the legendary Simpson Desert twice on this in-depth and un-hurried desert expedition, combining iconic dune tracks, remote landscapes, and premium outback hospitality.


Expedition Overview

The Double Crossing: Traverse the classic French, QAA, WAA, and Rig Road tracks west, then return east via the  remote Madigan Line.

Iconic Landmarks: Relax at Dalhousie Springs, conquer Big Red dune, and explore vibrantly coloured sand dunes and spinifex grasslands.

Key Inclusions: Cabin accommodation and dinner at the Birdsville Hotel to start, 2 nights at Mt Dare, and luxury hotel rooms, dinner, and breakfast at the Birdsville Hotel to finish.

The Itinerary

Leg 1 - Birdsville to Mt Dare: Spend 5 days mastering dune driving across the southern tracks and iconic lines, culminating in a relaxing soak at Dalhousie Springs, then recharge for 2 nights at the legendary Mt Dare Hotel.

Leg 2 - The Madigan Line:  Take on the remote Madigan Line spending another 5 days in untouched desert wilderness.

Leg 3 - Big Red & Birdsville: Rejoin the QAA Line for your final camp, conquer Big Red dune, grab lunch at the famous Birdsville Bakery, and celebrate with a hot shower and dinner, bed and breakfast at the iconic Birdsville Hotel.

Guided by an Expert
Real Track Experience. Deep Bush Knowledge.
Every Australian 4X4 Treks expedition is personally planned and guided by Tony. By pairing over 20 years of hands-on off-road experience with formal 4WD trainer, wildlife and tourism qualifications, he turns challenging desert tracks into safe, relaxed, and genuinely fascinating journeys.

What are the Vehicle Requirements?

• This tour is not suitable for off-road camper trailers.
• A high clearance 4X4 vehicle with low range.
• Upgraded, heavy duty suspension - even on a new vehicle.
• Light Truck construction (LT) tyres with over 70% tread remaining and not more than 5 years old (second spare recommended)
• Fuel range of 900 km (max. distance from Mt Dare to Birdsville is about 780km depending on the route we take, however, fuel consumption increases markedly if wet and muddy conditions are encountered).
• 60L water storage.
• Good quality UHF radio base set (Uniden, GME or Oricom recommended) fitted into your vehicle by a UHF specialist installer, and a substantial aerial - 6.6dbi, 1 metre in length, with large 30mm base - not the slim springy ones. These will break on the corrugations.
• Good quality sand flag with lynch pin base. Not a hose fitting type, and not the spring base type. Bushranger flag from ARB recommended.
• Tyre deflator and tyre pressure gauge.
• Tyre pressure monitors - TPMS.
• Good quality air compressor.
• Some spares are required to be carried (filters, belt etc). A full list is provided on booking.
• A Mechanical Checklist is to be completed by a qualified mechanic and returned at least 2 weeks prior to the start of the tour.
• A Medical Form is to be completed by each person in your vehicle and carried with you in your vehicle in case of emergency.
• A tent or camp set up that is EASY TO PUT UP AND TAKE DOWN. This should take no longer than 15 mins each time. This will make or break your trip. An awning tent connected to your vehicle in not recommended.
• Vehicle Insurance - make sure your insurance company covers your vehicle for off-road driving. NRMA/RACQ etc will not recover your vehicle from remote tracks. Only Club 4X4 Insurance will cover your vehicle recovery costs in remote situations. There is an option with Club 4X4 to get up to $30,000 for remote recoveries. (Our vehicle is insured with Club 4X4 but we are not sponsored by them).
• Travel Insurance - as with any holiday, you should get travel insurance in case of unforeseen circumstances (such as remote medical evacuation). 

Where can I get Fuel and Supplies?

Fuel is available along our route, however, you will need to have a fuel range of 900 km in case of wet weather. All care will be taken to check in advance whether fuel is available where we are going. It is advisable to carry an extra 20L of fuel for contingencies. Water and food is also available in these locations but fresh food is not always available and variety is limited.

Can I hire a 4WD vehicle and gear?

Yes. Please contact Overlander 4WD Hire directly to discuss hire options www.overlanderadventures.com.au 

Fuel Consumption Chart

Indicative desert driving fuel consumption is as follows (NB. this can increase markedly in wet and muddy conditions):
- Diesel utes eg. D-Max, Hilux, Ranger, BT-50 use 15 - 18L/100km
- Landrover Discovery 4 uses about 18L diesel/100km
- Diesel V8 Landcruisers use about 19L/100km
- Diesel V8 200 Series Landcruiser uses 20 - 22L/100km
- Petrol Nissan Patrol Y62 uses close to 28L/100km

Where can I store my caravan or camper trailer for the duration of the tour?

You can store your van or camper trailer at Windorah or Birdsville and collect it at the end of the tour . You can organise this directly with the Windorah Western Star Hotel or the Caravan Park in Birdsville (fees apply) . Note that the road to Birdsville from Windorah involves 280km of dirt road.

What happens if there is flooding or road closures that affect our tour?

We have alternative itineraries in the event of flooding or track closures, so the tour will go ahead. It is not always possible to run tours as planned in the Outback and desert. This is all part of travel in these remote areas.
